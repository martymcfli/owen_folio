The Vision

Trail Hazers was built around one idea: outdoor apparel that doesn't make you choose between performance and style. Positioned at the intersection of trail culture and everyday streetwear, the brand targeted a growing audience of hikers, trail runners, and weekend explorers who wanted gear that looked as good off the mountain as on it.

The opportunity sat in a clear market gap — premium outdoor brands prioritized technical specs over aesthetics, while fashion-forward labels lacked credibility in the outdoor space. Trail Hazers was designed to own that middle ground.

Building the Product

Product development was handled end-to-end — from sourcing and sampling through to final production. Each piece was selected with a focus on material quality, fit, and versatility: apparel that could transition from trailhead to city without looking like it was trying too hard.

Product Selection & Sourcing Identified and vetted suppliers, managed sampling rounds, and finalized production specs to balance quality and margin.
Brand Identity System Built the full visual identity in-house: wordmark, secondary marks, color palette, typography, and brand guidelines used across all touchpoints.
Collection Architecture Structured the product lineup into seasonal collections to drive repeat purchase, create launch moments, and support a content calendar.

E-Commerce & Operations

The brand launched on a fully optimized Shopify storefront built for conversion from day one. Product taxonomy, page structure, and checkout flow were designed around reducing friction and increasing average order value — not just getting traffic in the door.

Shopify Storefront Custom-configured store with conversion-focused product pages, collection architecture, upsell logic, and a streamlined checkout experience.
Fulfillment & Operations Automated order routing, inventory tracking, and fulfillment workflows to maintain speed and accuracy as order volume scaled.
Growth Channels Email flows, SMS campaigns, and organic social content built to drive repeat purchase and brand loyalty — not just first-touch transactions.

The Exit

Trail Hazers was successfully sold following a period of brand development and operational build-out. The acquisition validated the brand equity built through product quality, identity, and customer experience — completing the full founder-to-exit lifecycle.

The process required packaging the business for transfer: documenting operational processes, supplier relationships, brand assets, and customer data in a format that enabled a clean handoff and immediate continuity for the buyer.
